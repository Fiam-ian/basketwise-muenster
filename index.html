A LITTLE CLARITY

A cheap basket should be a complete basket.

We compare eligible whole-pack purchases using one product per required item. Equivalent duplicate list items are combined. Mixing different pack sizes or products for the same item is outside this prototype's comparison. Missing items never count as free. Refundable Pfand is included in checkout totals and shown separately.

This prototype uses fictional prices and straight-line trip estimates. A future live comparison will need verified sources and walking routes.
